# Open Research Questions & Hypotheses

- **RQ1 (Domain Shift):** How much does model F1-score degrade when transferring from clean laboratory reference slides to noisy outdoor optical trap imagery?
- **RQ2 (Focal Plane Sensitivity):** Can multi-focal z-stack fusion improve classification accuracy on closely related taxa (e.g., Birch vs. Alder) compared to single-plane 2D bright-field images?
- **RQ3 (Uncertainty Calibration):** Does temperature scaling or Monte Carlo Dropout reliably flag out-of-distribution debris (soot, microplastics) so parents do not receive false-positive allergy alerts?
